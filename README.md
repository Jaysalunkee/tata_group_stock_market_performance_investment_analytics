# Tata Group Stock Market Performance & Investment Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

**Tata Group Stock Market Performance & Investment Analytics** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing the stock market performance of selected Tata Group companies. The project uses **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to clean, explore, analyze, and visualize historical stock market data.

The objective is to identify **price trends, returns, risk, volatility, and investment performance** across major Tata Group companies and provide meaningful insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze historical stock price performance of Tata Group companies.
* Compare the performance of selected Tata stocks.
* Calculate and analyze daily returns.
* Measure stock risk and volatility.
* Identify market trends and price movements.
* Compare investment performance across companies.
* Build an interactive dashboard for financial decision-making.

---

## 🏢 Companies Analyzed

The project focuses on selected major Tata Group companies, including:

* Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
* Tata Motors
* Tata Steel
* Titan Company
* Tata Consumer Products
* Tata Power
